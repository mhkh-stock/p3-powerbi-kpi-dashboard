# Insights — NoorMart KPI dashboard (SAMPLE data, Jan–Jun 2026)

Totals: sales PKR 11,804,808 · profit PKR 5,360,644 · margin 45.4% ·
1,200 orders · AOV PKR 9,837. All figures verified by `src/checks.py`.

## Findings

1. **Furniture is the profit engine.** Furniture = PKR 4,112,441 (34.8% of
   sales); the top two products are both furniture (Office Chair Ergo
   PKR 1,591,488; Study Table Wooden PKR 1,185,509). Fashion is the
   smallest category at PKR 1,331,934 (11.3%) despite having 4 products.
2. **The year is stable, April is a one-month dip.** Monthly sales stay
   in a PKR 1.77M–2.08M band; April (PKR 1,773,350) is the low, May
   (PKR 2,057,292) recovers almost to January's peak (PKR 2,083,714).
   Nothing here reads as a falling trend.
3. **Geography is concentrated.** Punjab (PKR 3,969,335, 33.6%) + Sindh
   (PKR 3,229,228, 27.4%) = 61% of sales; Balochistan is only
   PKR 867,345 (7.3%). Channel split: In-Store 51.8%, Online 32.6%,
   WhatsApp Order 15.6%.

## 3 recommendations (what a manager would do Monday)

1. **Protect furniture stock first** — especially the Office Chair Ergo.
   A stock-out on the top product costs more than on any other line.
2. **Diagnose April before acting on it** — check stock, pricing and
   footfall for that month; do not cut a category on one soft month
   when May already recovered.
3. **Grow WhatsApp ordering** — the cheapest growth lever in this
   sample: lifting it from 15.6% toward Online's 32.6% needs no new
   store, only ordering + fulfilment that already exists.
